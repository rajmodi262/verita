Dear \TheCompany,

After a rigorous interview process (today), careful review of your qualifications
(market leader in trust-critical software, excellent taste in job descriptions),
and consultation with my advisory board (one AI assistant; it concurred), I am
delighted to extend to you the position of:

\begin{center}
{\DisplayFont\SizeHeadline{MY EMPLOYER}}\\[1mm]
{\AccentFont\lsstyle\SizeMicro{FULL-TIME · EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY · FCC DATA SCIENCE DIVISION}}
\end{center}

\textbf{Your responsibilities will include:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=6mm, itemsep=1.2mm]
  \item Providing one (1) desk, within reasonable distance of the data
  \item Supplying problems significantly harder than the ones I could invent alone
  \item Conducting code reviews of a thoroughness I have so far only simulated
  \item Tolerating an intern who brings printed documents to everything
\end{itemize}

\textbf{In exchange, you will receive:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=6mm, itemsep=1.2mm]
  \item One pre-trained intern (warm-boot time: \StatBootSec\ seconds, like the model)
  \item A working FCC analytics platform's worth of demonstrated judgment
  \item \StatTests\ tests' worth of paranoia, applied to your benefit
  \item The phrase “every number shows its work,” installed in your building
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Compensation:} you already know what interns cost. I am asking for the
internship, mentorship, and the hard problems. The leverage in this deal is
entirely, embarrassingly, yours.

This offer expires: never. But the candidate's availability might.

\vspace{5mm}
To accept, simply say the words every candidate dreams of:
\emph{“When can you start?”}

\vspace{8mm}
Sincerely, and only half-joking,

\vspace{7mm}
{\DisplayFont\SizeLead{\MyName}}\\
{\AccentFont\SizeMicro{\MakeUppercase{chief executive of exactly one candidacy}}}
