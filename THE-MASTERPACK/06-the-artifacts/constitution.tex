\article{20.9}{ARTICLE I — ON USER EXPERIENCE}
  {The first hour of analytical labour, being repetitive and beneath the dignity of
   the analyst, shall be abolished. One file drop shall summon a dashboard; said
   dashboard shall obey the user's hand in all matters of arrangement.}

\article{18.5}{ARTICLE II — ON DATA INTEGRITY}
  {No metric shall be fabricated, hardcoded, nor conjured by chance. Every number
   displayed shall be computed from the user's own data at the moment of asking,
   and shall, upon petition, confess the exact formula of its making.}

\article{16.1}{ARTICLE III — ON GRACEFUL FAILURE}
  {When errors arise — and they shall — the system shall fail as a gentleman:
   with a clear message, a proper status code, and no stack trace exposed to
   public view. The emergency exits shall remain lit at all times.}

\article{13.7}{ARTICLE IV — ON AI COLLABORATION}
  {The Machine shall narrate; the Engines shall compute. No output of the Machine
   shall ship unverified, and the judgment of the human shall bind in all disputes.
   The division of their labours shall be published openly, without shame.}

\article{11.3}{ARTICLE V — ON DOCUMENTATION}
  {That which is built and not documented shall be deemed not built. Every claim
   shall carry its evidence; every limit shall be confessed before the asking;
   every map shall mark its dragons by name.}

\article{8.9}{ARTICLE VI — ON FUTURE IMPROVEMENTS}
  {Version Two is hereby acknowledged a citizen in waiting — its engines (SHAP,
   XGBoost, Optuna) already resident within requirements.txt — and shall be
   admitted upon the same oath: honest selection, held-out judgment, work shown.}
