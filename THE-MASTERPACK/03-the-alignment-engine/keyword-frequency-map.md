# Keyword Frequency Map

*Which JD words carry the most decision weight, how often the domain repeats them,
and the exact Verita counter-phrase to echo back. Interviewers unconsciously score
candidates higher when the candidate's vocabulary matches the JD's. Use their words.*

## FREQUENCY DISCIPLINE

- Tier 1 words: aim to say each **3+ times** across the interview, naturally.
- Tier 2: **once well** beats thrice forced; each has exactly one rehearsed sentence.
- The phrase **"every number shows its work"** is MY keyword, not theirs — deploy it
  exactly twice: once in the opener, once in the close. Repetition at the bookends is
  how a phrase becomes the thing they remember at dinner.

## ANTI-KEYWORDS (words to avoid in this building)

| Avoid | Because | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "black box" (about my own work) | The thing they fear | "glass box" / "auditable" |
| "hacky" / "quick and dirty" | Liability culture hears risk | "scoped deliberately" |
| "the AI did X" | Ownership ambiguity | "I used AI to accelerate X; I verified it by Y" |
| "probably / I think it works" | Deposition-grade clarity or nothing | "it's tested — 82 in CI" |
| "disrupt" | Wrong industry's poetry | "make defensible" |
