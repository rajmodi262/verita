# Print Instructions

*Paper is part of the design. A masterpiece on 70gsm office paper is a flyer.
Budget version noted per item — the package degrades gracefully, like the product.*

## The print run

| Document | Paper | Sides | Binding | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hr-masterpack.pdf` | A4 120gsm matte | duplex | spiral coil OR heavy clip | **2** | one per panel side; cover page IS the poster — no extra cover |
| `tech-masterpack.pdf` | A4 120gsm matte | duplex | spiral coil OR heavy clip | **2** | same |
| `technical-appendix.pdf` | A4 100gsm | duplex | corner staple | **1** | stays in YOUR folder until someone goes past page 10 |
| `cover-letter-insert.pdf` | A4 100–120gsm | single | loose, on top of HR bundle | **2** | the literal first page they touch |
| `one-pager.pdf` | A4 **160gsm** | single | loose | **4** | the leave-behind; heaviest paper of the pack — weight = keep-me signal |
| `fake-newspaper.pdf` | A4 90gsm, **cream/ivory if available** | **single** | folded once, horizontally | **2** | the mid-interview surprise; the fold makes it an object |
| `business-card.pdf` | **300gsm+** card | duplex (short edge) | cut to crop marks | **5+** | print shop job; ask for matte lamination if cheap |
| `desk-poster.pdf` | **A3** 160gsm | single | **rolled, never folded** | **1** | rubber band; hand it over at the close if the room earned it |
| `constitution.pdf` | A4 120gsm cream | single | loose | **1** | lives inside the HR bundle's back pocket or handed with the poster |
| `project-receipt.pdf` | A4 80gsm (thin is correct here — it's a receipt) | single | trimmed to the receipt column if you're keen | **2** | tuck into the tech bundle |
| `offer-letter.pdf` | A4 plain | single | folded in an envelope, unsealed | **1** | the contingent closing bit; stays hidden unless earned |

## Print-shop checklist (say these words)

- "PDF/A export, **no scaling — 100% / actual size**" (TikZ full-bleeds die under "fit to page")
- "Color, not grayscale" (the package is built on a color system)
- "120gsm for the booklets, 160 for the single sheets, 300+ for the cards"
- For the A3 poster: "no lamination" (glare kills wall-pin charm)

## Budget fallback (still excellent)

Everything on A4 90gsm office color printing, clip-bound, EXCEPT: keep the
one-pager on 160gsm and the business cards on real card stock. Those two carry the
physical-quality signal; the rest carries it in the design.

## Assembly order (the physical stack you carry)

1. Cover letter (loose, top)
2. HR masterpack ×2
3. Tech masterpack ×2
4. One-pagers ×4 (between the bundles)
5. Folded newspaper ×2 (inner pocket — surprise asset)
6. Technical appendix (your folder only)
7. Constitution + receipt (back pocket)
8. Offer letter (envelope, very back)
9. Poster tube/roll (separate, in hand)
10. Business cards (jacket pocket — never the bag)

Everything also lives on your phone as PDFs. The folder is the show; the phone is
the understudy.
