# Morning-Of Checklist

*Short on purpose. The work is done; today is delivery.*

## Before leaving (15 min)

- [ ] Breakfast you've eaten before — nothing experimental, caffeine at normal dose
- [ ] The stack: bundles, one-pagers, newspaper, cards in jacket, poster roll
- [ ] Laptop + charger + the two CSVs
- [ ] Phone: PDFs present, ringer OFF, hotspot ready
- [ ] One look in the mirror; say the one-word answer out loud: **"Auditable."**

## In transit (the only study window — 20 min max)

- [ ] Read `rapid-fire-deck.md` once, both sides
- [ ] Read the FINAL FIVE twice
- [ ] Then put it away and look out the window. Marination beats cramming.

## Arrival (15 min early)

- [ ] Walk one block loop — convert the cortisol to motion
- [ ] Two physiological sighs in the corridor (double nose inhale, long mouth
      exhale) — NOT in the doorway
- [ ] Read the room in the first ten seconds → choose Opener A / B / C
- [ ] Bundle in hand, not in bag

## The last thing before the door

One sentence, internal, true:

> *The work is real. The chain is sealed. Every number shows its work —
> including me.*

Walk in at 70% speed.

— *(after: eat something, walk, write down the three surprising questions, stop
replaying. See `energy-management.md`. The chain doesn't re-open.)*
